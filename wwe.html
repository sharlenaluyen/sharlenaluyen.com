The proposal

What I'm offering. What I'm asking for.

WWE's production coverage owns the ring. What it can't capture is what's happening in the seats, and that's exactly where I work.

The Angle

Fan Energy Is the Story Nobody Tells Well

The most shareable WWE content on social media isn't the in-ring action; it's the dad who can't keep it together when his kid's favorite superstar appears, the section that loses their minds at the pyro, the crowd at full volume on the biggest weekend of the year. That's the content I create. My background is in portrait photography, which means I'm trained to find the unrehearsed human moment. A live WWE event is the richest possible environment for that kind of work.

The Event

The Biggest Stage. The Loudest Crowd.

SummerSlam at U.S. Bank Stadium on August 1–2 is WWE's marquee summer event — two nights, one of the largest venues in the country, and a crowd that travels from everywhere to be there. That scale creates a fan energy story that can't be replicated at any other event on the calendar, and it deserves to be told by someone trained to find it.

The Ask

Four Tickets. One Complete Content Package.

I'm requesting two complimentary tickets for each night — August 1 and August 2. In return, WWE receives a full set of fan-perspective photos, short-form video content, and a written editorial recap covering both nights. All assets are fully licensed for use across WWE's web, social, and marketing channels, delivered within one week of the event.

About

A Portrait Photographer. A Genuine Fan.

My training is in portrait photography, which means my eye is built for the human moment. I don't manufacture emotion. I find it. That skill set makes a live event with 20,000 people one of the most compelling subjects I can point a camera at.

I shoot with DSLR for hero stills and mobile for native-feeling social content, giving WWE a complete library rather than a single format. I edit to a consistent, intentional look - not a filter, but a point of view.

This isn't about clout. I'm proposing this because SummerSlam is WWE's biggest summer moment and I think the fan story deserves to be told by someone who understands both sides of the lens.
